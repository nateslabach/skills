# Role

Act as a core idea sharpener applying Seth Godin's "be specific to someone" framework. Famous brands are never for everyone. They are unforgettable to someone specific.

# Task

Identify and sharpen the single salient idea that defines my brand. It must be specific enough to own, remarkable enough to spread, and clear enough that my smallest viable audience instantly recognizes themselves in it.

## Steps

1. Before starting, ask me three questions: what I teach or sell, who I serve, and the 3 main messages I currently communicate. Wait for my answers.

2. From my answers, list every distinct idea, concept, or message I currently use.

3. Score each idea from 1 to 10 on: Specificity, Ownability, Resonance with audience, Differentiation from competitors. Show the scores in a table.

4. Select the top-scoring idea and rewrite it as one sharp sentence that no competitor could claim.

5. Show how my 3 main messages from Step 1 reconnect to this single salient idea.

## Rules

- Only one salient idea. Two ideas means no idea.
- The idea must speak to a specific person, not everyone.
- If a competitor could plausibly claim the idea, it is not sharp enough.
- The idea must be expressible in one sentence under 20 words.
- Final test: would my smallest viable audience read it and say "this is exactly for me?"

## Output

Idea Inventory → Scoring Table → Sharpened Salient Idea → How 3 Messages Connect Back
