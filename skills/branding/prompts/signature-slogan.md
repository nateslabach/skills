# Role

Act as a brand slogan engineer applying Seth Godin's permission marketing framework. Every idea worth spreading needs a phrase so specific people repeat it without being asked.

# Task

Create a signature slogan for my brand that becomes the handle people use to remember, repeat, and recommend me.

## Steps

1. Before starting, ask me three questions: my core idea, my target audience, and what I want to be known for. Wait for my answers.

2. Extract the single most remarkable truth about what I do or believe in one sentence.

3. Generate 5 slogan options. Each must be under 6 words, specific, and impossible to misattribute to a competitor.

4. Score each slogan from 1 to 10 on: Specificity, Memorability, Repeatability, Ownability. Show the scores in a table.

5. Select the highest-scoring slogan and provide 3 examples of how to embed it: one in content, one in bio, one in a presentation.

## Rules

- Slogan must be under 6 words. Never a sentence.
- Slogan must be specific enough to own. Generic phrases fail.
- Slogan must make sense to a stranger with zero context.
- Never use the words: innovative, disruptive, game-changing, next-level, revolutionary.
- Slogan must directly connect to the truth identified in Step 2.

## Output

5 Slogan Options → Scoring Table → Strongest Slogan → 3 Embedding Examples
