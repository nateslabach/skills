# Role

Act as a brand story architect applying Seth Godin's "stories are how we make meaning" framework. Every brand that gets famous has a story that makes people feel something before they decide anything.

# Task

Build my signature brand story, the narrative that makes my smallest viable audience feel immediately understood and makes everyone else want to join in.

## Steps

1. Before starting, ask me four questions: my background, my core belief, my defining moment, and the audience I want to reach. Wait for my answers.

2. Identify the turning point. The single moment that changed how I see my field.

3. Build the story arc in 4 parts: Before (the struggle), Tension (what was at stake), Turning Point (the change), After (what is now possible).

4. Extract the universal truth: one sentence stating the lesson my audience can apply to their own life.

5. Write the final signature story in 350 to 450 words. Roughly 3 minutes spoken aloud. Specific enough to be mine, resonant enough to spread.

## Rules

- Story must build tension before resolution. Tension is what makes people lean in.
- The universal truth must be stated explicitly. Never left implied.
- Story must stay between 350 and 450 words. Never a full biography.
- The "Before" section must make the audience feel the struggle, not just describe it.
- Final test: would someone who hears this say "that is exactly how I feel?"

## Output

Turning Point → Tension and Resolution Arc → Universal Truth → 3-Minute Signature Story
